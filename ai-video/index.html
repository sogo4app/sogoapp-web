Make AI videos in SogoApp with Seedance

By the SogoApp team · Sogoapp LLC · Updated November 2026

SogoApp lets you turn a short description or a photo into a video and post it as a reel. The video is generated by Seedance, the video model from BytePlus (ModelArk). This page explains how it works, what you need and what it costs.

How it works

  1. Open AI Video in SogoApp. Each video gets its own chat, so you can refine it step by step ("make it night", "slower camera").
  2. Describe the scene or pick a photo. Write what should happen, or start from one of your images.
  3. Choose the model, quality and length. The app shows which options each Seedance model supports.
  4. Seedance generates the clip on your own BytePlus account, usually in one to three minutes.
  5. Post it as a reel. Add text in the editor and publish. Every AI reel carries a visible "Made with AI" label so viewers know how it was made.

What you need

SogoApp does not resell video credits. You connect your own BytePlus account, and the generation is billed (or covered by free tokens) there. Set-up takes a few minutes:

  1. Create a BytePlus account at byteplus.com.
  2. In ModelArk, turn on a Seedance model (for example Seedance 1.0 Pro).
  3. Create an API key and paste it into SogoApp, or tap Set up for me in the app and follow the guided steps. The full walkthrough with screenshots is at app.sogoapps.com/ai.

Your key stays on your phone and is sent only to BytePlus when you generate a video. You can remove it from the app at any time.

Model names, limits and prices are set by BytePlus and can change. The app shows the current estimate before you generate.

What it costs

New BytePlus accounts get free tokens, which cover many short videos on Seedance 1.0 Pro. After that, BytePlus charges per video by length and resolution, and the app shows an estimate in US dollars before each generation. SogoApp adds no fee on top.

Rules for AI reels

  • AI reels follow the same Community Guidelines as every other post.
  • Do not create videos of real people without their consent, and do not pass AI video off as real footage.
  • The "Made with AI" label cannot be removed.

Questions

Is SogoApp affiliated with BytePlus?
SogoApp integrates the BytePlus ModelArk API so you can use Seedance inside the app. BytePlus and Seedance are trademarks of their owners.
Do I need a credit card?
Not to start. The BytePlus free tokens are enough to try it. BytePlus asks for payment only when you go beyond them.
Where are my videos stored?
Drafts stay on your phone. A video becomes public only when you post it as a reel.
